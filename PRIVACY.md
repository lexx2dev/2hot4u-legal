# Privacy Policy

2hot4u stores only what it needs to run temporary voice rooms.

## Data we store
- **Server settings:** server ID, hub/category/log/announcement channel IDs, role IDs, room-name template and appearance options.
- **Rooms:** channel ID, owner's user ID, room name, limits and lock state.
- **Permission lists:** user IDs you trust or block in your room.
- **Premium status** per server, if applicable.

We do not read or store message content, and we do not collect email addresses, IP addresses or voice audio.

## How it is used
Solely to provide the bot's features. Data is not sold or shared with third parties.

## Retention and deletion
- Room data is removed when the room is deleted.
- When the bot is removed from a server, all of that server's data is deleted automatically.
- To request deletion of your data, join the support server (https://discord.gg/VtArUDSBQb) and contact the bot owner.

## Changes
This policy may be updated; the current version is always in this repository.
